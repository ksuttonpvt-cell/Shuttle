Set up your shuttle site

A few questions and you're ready to go.

Your manager account

You'll use this to sign in and run the site. You can add staff afterwards.

It's shown in the window on the computer running the site (and in its log), so only the person who installed it can set it up.

Your business

A shuttle service is any other business with a shuttle: a hotel, airport parking, an office, events…

Used in “bring you to the ___”. For example: dealership, hotel, office, terminal.

Where does your shuttle start?

Pick your address from the list, or tap the map to mark the spot.

Your colors

Used for buttons and the header on every page. You can add your logo, an app icon and a banner later in Manager → Settings.
