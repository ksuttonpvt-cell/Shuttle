One app, three easy screens

Everyone sees just what they need, and one big button for what's next.

Your service desk

Book a pick up or drop off in four taps. The queue sorts itself: urgent and late rides first, then soonest, then closest.

  • A meter says how long the shuttle is running right now
  • Pick a ride to assign a driver, edit, or send the tracking link
  • Customers back at your shop are counted, ready for their ride home

Your drivers

One card for the next stop, with Navigate, Call and Text, and one big button for what's next: on the way, I'm here, on board, done.

  • New rides near them pop up to take or pass
  • The best order for several stops, with arrival times
  • Lock-screen alerts, even with the app closed

Your customers

A text with a link: the van on a live map, minutes away, the driver and the plate. Nothing to download.

  • "I'm running late" and cancel, right from the link
  • "Your shuttle is here", with how long the driver can wait
  • Your name and logo, not ours

The name
“Turas Lúbtha” is Irish for the looped journey: out to your customer and back again, every ride.

Say it: TOO-rus LOOB-ha. Purple for the Scottish thistle, green for Ireland, teal for the sea between.

Questions

Do my drivers need an app?

No. It opens in the phone's browser and can be added to the home screen like an app. A phone app for the app stores is on the way.

Can it use my own website address?

Yes, like shuttle.yourshop.com, and you can add a "Request a ride" button to your own website.

Hosted or on my own computer?

Either. We can host it for you, or you can install it on a computer at your business for a lower price.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes, everywhere: the app follows each phone's setting, and anyone can switch it under their name.

What about customer privacy?

You choose how long customer details are kept (30 days by default); then they're cleared automatically.

We're a repair shop, not a dealership. Will it fit?

Yes. Repair shops, body shops and hotels use the same screens, with your own word for your place.
