The driver app

Your runs, your stops and the live map, right on your phone. One app for every business on Turas Lúbtha: sign in once and you land in your own.

1

Put it on your phone

Android: tap the button, then Install.

Open turaslubtha.com/driver in Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone: open turaslubtha.com/driver in Safari, tap Share ⬆︎, then Add to Home Screen and Add.

✅ You're in the app.

2

Sign in

Open Turas Driver from your home screen and sign in with the email and password your manager gave you. It remembers you after that.

Sign in now

3

Allow location and notifications

Location shows customers and the desk where the van is. Notifications tell you about new rides, even when the app is closed.

On a computer?

Scan this with your phone's camera to open this page there.

🚐 Set things up before your shift. The app is made so you don't need to touch it while driving; pull over before you tap.
