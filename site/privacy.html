Privacy

Turas Lúbtha helps businesses run a customer shuttle. This page explains what information the service handles and how it's protected.

Who decides what's collected

Each business that uses Turas Lúbtha (for example a dealership or repair shop) decides what it records about its own customers and staff. Turas Lúbtha processes that information on the business's behalf.

Customers riding the shuttle

  • Name, phone number, pick-up or drop-off address and ride notes, entered by the business or by the customer when requesting a ride.
  • Used only to run the ride: tracking links, texts about the ride, and the driver's directions.
  • Cleared automatically after a period the business chooses (30 days by default). Ride counts and times without personal details are kept for reports.

Staff and drivers

  • Name, work email, phone and sign-in details.
  • A driver's location is shared only while the driver page is open, to show the van on the map and estimate arrival times.

What we never do

We don't sell personal information or use it for advertising.
